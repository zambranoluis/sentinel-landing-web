## How it works

### More visibility into what is happening More precision on where to act

Sentinel helps turn large volumes of video into prioritised information. It monitors configured feeds, flags relevant events and presents the context people need to review, decide and respond.

## Capabilities

Sentinel adapts detection models and rules to the operational context of each site, supporting security, loss prevention, workplace safety and operational visibility.

### 01 — Supermarkets and retail

Identify configured events related to product concealment, cash-to-pocket activity, cashier monitoring and people flow, then present them for review with visual context.

## FAQ

> **Default interaction state:** All FAQ accordion items are collapsed/closed on initial page load.

### The essentials before you get started

Direct answers about compatibility, data, alerts and operation.

### Do I need to buy new cameras?

Not necessarily. Sentinel can work with compatible existing camera systems. The site assessment confirms compatibility and identifies any changes required before deployment.

### Does my footage go to the cloud?

Sentinel supports deployments in which processing and operational data remain within the client's local infrastructure. The exact data flow, storage and alert configuration are confirmed for each implementation.

### How fast are the alerts?

Alert timing depends on the detection model, configuration, network and deployment conditions. Sentinel is designed to flag configured events as they are detected and provide the available context for review.

### What can Sentinel detect?

Sentinel can support capabilities such as facial recognition, cash-to-pocket, cashier monitoring, people flow, restricted-area monitoring, shoplifting/pilferage detection, licence plate recognition, custom object detection, weapon detection and other models configured for the implementation. The current catalogue and availability are confirmed during assessment.

### I already have security personnel. Why do I need Sentinel?

Sentinel is designed to support trained personnel, not replace them. It can continuously monitor configured feeds and direct attention to events that require review, while your team remains responsible for judgement and response.

### What happens if my internet connection goes down?

Where Sentinel is configured for local processing, core detection can continue without relying on cloud processing. Delivery to external alert channels may depend on connectivity. The exact behaviour is confirmed for each deployment.

### What happens if the equipment fails?

System-health monitoring, recovery behaviour and support conditions depend on the deployed hardware and configuration. Support and replacement arrangements are defined in the implementation proposal.

### Am I locked into a fixed contract?

Commercial terms are defined in the written proposal after the site assessment. You can review the scope, pricing and terms before deployment.
