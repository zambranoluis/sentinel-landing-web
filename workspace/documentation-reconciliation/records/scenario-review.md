## Cross-case review

The same paths retain proportional planning through [PLANS](../../../AGENTS/PLANS.md), independent work while an answer is pending, explicit stop boundaries, honest failed/unverified checks and preservation of user-managed processes. A straightforward answer or read-only review creates no implementation authorization. Current PRODUCT/DESIGN/briefs explain the accepted baseline without requiring a deleted historical plan or machine-local server. All five cases reach their applicable owners without the retired adoption guide.

This confirms written routing and consistency, not empirical compliance by another agent. Future loading/behaviour diagnosis uses the active host's evidence and remains a separately scoped task.
