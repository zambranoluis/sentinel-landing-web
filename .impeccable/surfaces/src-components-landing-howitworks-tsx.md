## Surface guidance

Large left-hand copy and a radial five-panel diagram surround the central cube at desktop. The diagram stacks below the text at intermediate widths; mobile puts the cube above a readable vertical wrapping workflow. Use semantic heading, paragraph and ordered-list content.

Keep section text and artwork server rendered with a narrow workflow client boundary receiving the unchanged cube. The supplied cube motion automatically pauses offscreen or when the document is hidden, without resetting position, and resumes when visible. Workflow playback controls never govern the cube. The bottom halo follows lower-row propagation, floats behind the cube and masks its silhouette; preserve the inner glow. All five icons and their circular rings use workflow cyan (`#05ddf1`). Existing navbar/footer links reach the section, close mobile navigation and focus the target.

Enhance the ordered list into native buttons after hydration. Cycle the five stages every 2.4s with 700ms accents and a reading hold. Desktop/tablet trace each active connection toward the cube, pulse its endpoint dots/icon and sweep short ring accents while preserving underlying lines and tile positions. Through 720px keep all five wrapping rows and add a slim cyan progression rail with a travelling accent. Border/tint feedback takes 200ms; pointer press uses a 120ms scale to 0.98.

Hover previews on fine pointers; keyboard focus overrides hover. Leaving restores manual selection or the interrupted automatic stage. Click/tap/Enter/Space hold a step (`aria-pressed`) until “Resume sequence” continues from it. The small “Pause sequence” / “Resume sequence” control beneath the composition affects only the workflow. Rapid selections cancel obsolete accents. Offscreen/hidden playback preserves its remaining hold. Keyboard selection and reduced motion settle immediately; reduced motion disables cycling/spatial effects and playback. Resizing preserves control identity, focus and selection while cancelling transient geometry. Without JavaScript show the complete static list/artwork without interaction controls. The separate demo's stage labels remain noninteractive.

Cyan/blue connectors and glows are local illustration details. This explanation adds no commercial CTA or operational certainty.
