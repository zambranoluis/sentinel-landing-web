## Surface guidance

Preserve the ten-section story: hero, workflow, capabilities, illustrative demo, deployment, benefits, plans, FAQ, final CTA and footer. Keep reference compositions, assets and exact approved content. The offer supports human review and response; detection artwork is not live evidence.

Internal section/industry navigation is available. Assessment, package and add-on actions remain disabled; contact/company/legal information remains text pending product-owned destinations and submission/privacy contracts. Do not invent routes or claims to create a conversion path.

The illustrative demo automatically cycles through three 3.6-second stages plus a two-second final review hold. Stage labels are noninteractive, with no playback, selection or report actions. Visibility suspends the current timeline. Reduced motion/no JavaScript preserves the static final review. FAQ keeps native disclosure access; deployment adds restrained, visible-content entry. The existing workflow cube retains its separate pause behaviour.
